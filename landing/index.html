Gootd

Lights out.
One hotkey.

Set your hotkey and the display and keyboard backlight cut out immediately — agents keep working. Press it again and everything is back where you left it.

$brew install --cask dexrfy0000/gootd/gootd && xattr -cr /Applications/Gootd.app

Runs on any Apple silicon Mac with macOS 13 or newer, and it’s free. Bind the hotkey to whatever you like. It stays dark until you press it again, or quit the app.
